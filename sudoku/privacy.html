Privacy Policy – Sudoku: All You Need

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how The Little Dynasty Games ("we", "us"), the developer and operator of Sudoku: All You Need (the "game"), handles information. We are responsible for the processing we determine as described here. Our privacy contact is kisskiraly79@gmail.com.

At a glance: you can play Sudoku offline without creating a game account. Progress, balances, skins and records are saved on your device. Your date of birth is used on the device to determine an age group; the full date is not saved or sent to our advertising provider. Google advertising and consent services can still process personal data when used.

1. The game and this policy

Sudoku: All You Need is an offline-capable Sudoku game being developed for Android and iOS. Availability and features may differ by platform. The current game includes Sudoku types and difficulty levels, Daily Sudoku, a local profile, progression, virtual currencies and cosmetic skins. Playing does not require your name, email address, telephone number, a game account or a social login. The current game does not offer developer-operated cloud saves, online player profiles or chat.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Selected puzzle types, difficulty and symbol settings, current puzzles, player entries, mistakes, elapsed time and completion state.
  • Daily Sudoku completion dates, progression, XP, level, score, statistics, streaks and best completion times.
  • Gold and Diamond balances, owned and equipped skins, and local transaction and reward references that prevent duplicate credits or purchases.
  • Your derived age group, confirmation that the date-of-birth step was completed, and save-recovery information.

These are local game records. The game does not upload these saves to a developer-operated server. Your operating system or device backup service may separately copy or restore app data according to your device settings. Advertising and consent services have their own data handling, described below; local saving does not mean that the entire app never connects to the internet.

3. Date of birth and younger players

On first use, the game asks for a year, month and day. The game calculates an age group on the device: under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or 18 and over. It stores only the group and the fact that this step was confirmed. The full date of birth is not retained in the game save, uploaded to us or passed to Google. It is cleared from the input fields after confirmation. This is a self-declared date, not identity verification.

You can enter a new date through Profile → Date of birth to correct the group used by the game. Until this step is confirmed, the game does not initialize its advertising service or request advertisements. The current implementation does not request rewarded ads for the under-13 group. For players aged 13–17, it applies Google's teen treatment and under-age-of-consent flag. Age-related flags, rather than the full date, are used to configure advertising and consent handling.

If you are a parent or guardian with concerns about a child's information, contact us. We will review the concern and take appropriate steps, including working with the relevant provider where necessary. Please do not send identity documents or unnecessary information about a child.

4. Google AdMob and optional rewarded ads

The game integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where ads are available and permitted, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad, for example to receive an additional Gold reward after completing a Sudoku. Watching is optional. Declining an ad does not remove the normal completion reward or prevent offline play.

Google's SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, advertising/app-set or other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and technical diagnostics or performance information. This supports advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. On iOS, it may also process crash and advertising information; identifiers depend on platform permissions and settings. The game does not request precise GPS location. Actual processing depends on the SDK, consent, device controls and ad configuration.

Privacy checks and, where allowed, ad preparation can connect to Google before you press the watch-ad button. Not watching an ad does not disable every service request. Non-personalized or limited ads can also involve data processing.

Read the Android SDK disclosure, iOS SDK disclosure, Google Privacy Policy and how Google handles information from apps using its services.

5. Consent and privacy choices

UMP checks whether a consent message or privacy-options entry point is required. Where applicable, it presents the messages configured for this game in AdMob. The game checks whether ad requests are permitted before initializing the advertising SDK and requesting an ad. When this check fails, the game leaves ads unavailable.

When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, open Profile → Ad privacy to review or change the available choices. This button is shown when required; its availability depends on Google's response. Consent to advertising-related processing is separate from choosing to watch a rewarded video. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it through the applicable privacy controls without affecting earlier lawful processing.

You may also manage advertising identifiers and preferences through your device and Google settings. Withdrawing consent or changing an identifier does not automatically delete all records a provider already holds. See Google's UMP information and Google's advertising information.

6. Purposes and legal grounds

Local records provide the requested game, preserve progress and preferences, calculate statistics and maintain consistent virtual rewards. Advertising supports optional monetization and the purposes in section 4. If you contact us, we use your correspondence to answer questions and handle support or privacy requests.

Where European or UK data-protection law applies, relevant grounds can include providing the requested service for necessary game functions, consent for advertising-related processing or device access where required, legitimate interests in responding to support requests and protecting the service where these do not override your rights, and applicable legal obligations. A legitimate-interest ground does not replace consent where consent is required. Google's privacy information explains its own processing and legal grounds.

7. Providers, sharing and international processing

Google receives information through its advertising and consent services. We do not send your Sudoku saves, balances or owned-skin inventory to Google as custom advertising data. The current game does not enable a separate Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or Unity Analytics service; Google's advertising services can perform their own measurement and diagnostics.

Google and providers involved in operating its services may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA or UK. Their privacy information explains applicable safeguards and transfer arrangements. We do not describe the game as collecting or sharing no personal data merely because its gameplay records are saved locally.

8. Virtual currencies and purchases

Gold and Diamond are virtual game balances. The current Shop uses Gold to obtain cosmetic skins; ownership and equipment choices are saved locally. The current game has no real-money in-app purchases, payment-card collection or active Remove Ads / No Ads purchase. If real-money purchases are introduced, we will review this policy and relevant store disclosures before that release.

9. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. Our email provider also handles the correspondence. Share only what is needed for your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details or identity documents in an initial support message.

10. Retention and security

Local records remain until app data is removed, subject to device backup and restore settings. The game also keeps local recovery and reward records. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed for the request and related follow-up, or for an applicable legal or security purpose. We do not set retention periods for Google's own advertising, consent or operational records; see Google's retention information.

Local app storage relies on your operating system's protections. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and any backup account. Google describes encryption in transit for the information its Mobile Ads SDK sends.

11. Removing data and making requests

The current game does not include an in-game delete-all-data button. To remove device-local records, use your device's controls to clear the game's storage where supported, or delete the app and its data. This can remove progress, currency, skins and records. Manage device/cloud backups separately if you do not want an old save restored. Removing local data does not delete information already held independently by Google or an email provider.

There is no developer game account or cloud-save account to delete. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase a save that exists only on your device. For information you sent directly to us, such as support correspondence, email the privacy contact below. Google's privacy information provides its own data-access and deletion channels.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us about information for which we are responsible. We may need proportionate information to verify a request and will respond within the period required by applicable law. Available rights and lawful exceptions depend on the processing and your jurisdiction.

13. Changes to this policy

We will review this policy when game features, SDKs or data practices change, including any future real-money purchases or online services. The updated policy will appear here with a new date. We will provide additional notice or obtain consent where required. Planned features are not statements that they already operate in the current game.
