Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how The Little Dynasty Games ("we", "us"), the developer and operator of Funny: 2 3 4 Player Games (the "game"), handles information. We are responsible for the processing we determine as described here. Our privacy contact is kisskiraly79@gmail.com.

At a glance: play locally on one device, without a game account. Game balances, settings and records are saved locally. Optional rewarded advertising uses Google services and can involve personal data. "Offline multiplayer" does not mean that advertising services never connect to the internet.

1. The game and this policy

The current Android game supports 2–4 people playing together on a single device. It does not offer online multiplayer, player accounts, sign-in, cloud profiles, chat or developer-operated cloud saving. The Android package identifier is com.thelittledynastygames.funny234playergames. An iOS version is planned; this policy does not imply that it is already available.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Shared Gold and Diamond balances, local reward transaction references and save-recovery data used to prevent duplicate rewards and recover a damaged save.
  • Game settings, including saved keyboard-control assignments where supported.
  • Local achievements of play such as best survival times, cleared courses and enemy-knockout records, including records for different player counts.

These are device-local game records, not online player profiles. We do not upload these game saves to a developer-operated server. Current match state and some scores are temporary and are not necessarily retained after leaving the game. Your operating system or device backup service may separately back up or restore app data according to your device settings.

3. Optional rewarded advertising

The game integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can choose to watch a rewarded advertisement for a virtual reward, currently for example +1 Diamond. Declining to watch a rewarded advertisement does not prevent local play. Virtual currency is not real money; the current version does not provide in-app purchases.

Google's advertising SDK can process network addresses, advertising and app-set identifiers, app/ad engagement events and technical performance or error information. A network address can reveal an approximate area; the game does not request GPS location. Processing can support ad delivery, personalization where permitted, reporting, service performance and abuse prevention. The information actually processed depends on device controls, consent, SDK behavior and ad configuration.

A network connection may be used to request privacy forms and, where permitted, prepare advertisements before you tap to watch a video. Choosing not to watch a video is not the same as disabling every SDK request. Non-personalized or limited advertisements can also involve data processing.

See the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps using its services.

4. Consent and privacy choices

UMP checks whether a privacy message or a privacy-options entry point is required. Where applicable, it presents choices based on the messages configured for this game in AdMob. The integration checks whether ad requests are permitted before initializing and requesting ads. The availability and content of these choices depend on your region and the applicable service requirements.

When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, open the game's Settings and choose Privacy Options (shown as ADATVÉDELMI BEÁLLÍTÁSOK in the current interface) to review or change your choices. This entry is shown when required. Consent to personalized advertising is separate from choosing to watch a rewarded video. You can withdraw consent through the available privacy controls; this does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

You may also manage advertising identifiers and ad preferences through Android/device and Google settings. These controls do not necessarily delete information already retained by a service provider. Read Google's UMP information for details about the consent system.

5. Purposes and legal grounds

We use local game data to provide the requested game, preserve progress and preferences, maintain rewards and avoid duplicate credit. Advertising-related processing supports optional monetization and the purposes described in section 3. If you contact us, we use your message to answer questions and handle privacy or technical requests.

Where European or UK data-protection law applies, relevant grounds can include performance of the requested service for necessary game functions, consent for advertising-related processing or device access where required, our legitimate interests in responding to support requests and protecting the service where those interests do not override your rights, and compliance with applicable legal obligations. Consent-required processing is not replaced by a claim of legitimate interest. Google's own legal grounds are explained in its privacy information.

6. Providers, sharing and international processing

Google receives information through its advertising and consent services. We do not operate a server that collects your local game saves, and the current game has no separate developer-added analytics or account service. Advertising services can still perform their own measurement and diagnostics. This policy does not promise that the game collects or shares no personal data.

Google and other providers involved in delivering their services may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA or UK. Their privacy information explains the safeguards and transfer mechanisms they use where required. Contact us with questions about processing for which we are responsible.

7. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. Our email provider also processes the correspondence. Please send only information necessary for your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details or identity documents in an initial support message.

8. Retention

Local game data remains on the device until it is cleared or removed, subject to device backup and restore settings. We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to resolve your request and related follow-up, or as required for an applicable legal or security purpose. We do not set the retention periods for Google's own advertising, consent or operational records. See Google's retention information for its practices.

9. Deleting data

To remove local saves and settings on Android, use the operating system's app-storage controls to clear the game's storage/data. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but device or cloud backups may retain or restore it; review those settings separately. Clearing only the app cache is not the same as clearing its data. Removing saves can permanently remove balances and records.

There is no game account to delete in the current version. For correspondence or other information we control, send a privacy request to our contact address. Clearing local storage does not automatically delete Google's records. Use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for records it controls; we can help explain the relevant route.

10. Security

Local saves use the application's device storage. Save-integrity checks help detect damage but are not encryption or a guarantee against access on a compromised device. Protect your device and backups. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise rights relating to information we control. We may need proportionate information to verify a request and will respond within the period required by applicable law. We cannot retrieve device-only saves from a developer cloud account, because none exists.

For information independently controlled by Google, its privacy policy explains its request channels. Your available rights and any lawful exceptions depend on the processing and your jurisdiction.

12. Children and intended audience

The game is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. An age rating is not a claim that parental authorization is unnecessary everywhere: local rules may require a parent or guardian's involvement for some teenagers. We do not ask players to create accounts or submit their date of birth.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal data through the game without any authorization required by law, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate steps, including working with the relevant provider where necessary. Do not include unnecessary information about the child.

13. Future features and changes

Online invitation codes, profiles, cloud services, a shop, in-app purchases and an iOS release are possible future developments, not descriptions of current data collection. We will review this policy and relevant notices before introducing features that change our data practices. The updated policy will appear here with a new date, with additional notice or consent where required.
