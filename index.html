Privacy Policy

Next Brainrot: Evolution

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Little Dynasty Games ("we", "us") handles information when you use Next Brainrot: Evolution (the "game"). Contact us at kisskiraly79@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

1. Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication

When you use Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication manages your game account. Account information may include your email address, Firebase user identifier (UID), Google account identifier, and basic profile information supplied by Google, such as your display name and profile picture. We use account identifiers to recognize your account and associate it with your cloud save. We do not receive your Google password.

2. Game progress and Firestore cloud saves

The game stores progress on your device and uses Cloud Firestore for cloud saves associated with your signed-in account. Saved data may include levels, unlocked content, in-game resources, upgrades, settings, and save timestamps. This information supports gameplay and saving or restoring progress. The exact saved values depend on the game features you use.

3. Advertising and consent

The game uses AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Depending on the SDK configuration, device settings, and applicable consent choices, Google may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, app activity, and diagnostic information. These data support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service reliability. IP addresses may be used to estimate a general location.

Personalized advertising depends on applicable consent requirements and settings. Where consent is required, it must be obtained before the relevant processing. Non-personalized or limited ads can still involve some data processing; they do not necessarily mean that no data is collected. Use the privacy or consent options presented in the game, where available, to review your choices. You can also manage advertising preferences through your Google account and device settings.

Read Google's advertising information and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

4. How information is used

  • Authenticate your account and provide cloud saving and restoration.
  • Maintain your game progress and preferences.
  • Provide advertising and support its measurement and security.
  • Respond to support messages, verify deletion requests, and address technical or security issues.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. Please share only what is necessary to handle your request.

5. Third-party services

The game relies on Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Google processes information in accordance with the terms and privacy rules applicable to those services. Processing may take place outside your country of residence.

6. Data retention

Account information and cloud saves are retained while needed to provide your account and cloud-save features, unless you request deletion. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and any related issues. If a legal or security need requires limited retention, the relevant information is retained only for that purpose and for as long as necessary. Service providers may apply separate retention periods to their own operational records. Local game data remains on your device until it is reset or removed.

7. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your game's Firebase Authentication account and its associated cloud save / Firestore data by following the instructions on our Account and Data Deletion page. We may confirm the request or verify account ownership before deleting data. Once deletion is completed, your deleted cloud progress cannot be restored.

Deleting your game account does not delete your Google account. Uninstalling the game or clearing local storage does not by itself delete your Firebase account or cloud save.

8. Security

We rely on Firebase's authentication and hosted infrastructure to support account and cloud-data security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Protect your Google account and device, and never send us your password or verification codes.

9. Children's privacy

Children's personal information requires special care. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the game without any authorization required by applicable law, contact us. We will review the situation and arrange deletion where appropriate. Please do not send identity documents or unnecessary information about the child in your initial message.

10. Your privacy requests

You may contact us to ask about your information or request access, correction, or deletion. Depending on your location, applicable law may provide additional rights, including the right to object to or restrict certain processing or complain to a data protection authority. We may need to verify account ownership to protect your information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date. Where applicable, additional notice or consent will be provided for material changes.
