Account and Data Deletion

Next Brainrot: Evolution

Use this page to request deletion of your Next Brainrot: Evolution game account and associated cloud-save data.

Request deletion by email

This is a static information page. It does not connect to Firebase or delete data automatically. The button opens your email app; you must send the message to submit your request.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not work, send an email to kisskiraly79@gmail.com with the subject Next Brainrot account deletion request.

1. What to include

  1. Send the email from the same email address you used for Google Sign-In in the game, whenever possible.
  2. State that you want your Next Brainrot: Evolution account and associated cloud-save data deleted.
  3. If you contact us from a different address, include the Google / Firebase account email address used in the game so we can identify the account. We may require an additional account-ownership check before acting.

If you can no longer access that email account, explain this in your message. Knowing an account's email address alone is not proof of ownership.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or identity documents. No reason for deletion is required.

2. What happens next

The Little Dynasty Games will review your request and may reply to confirm your intention or verify that you own the account. Once verified, we will process deletion of the game account and associated cloud-save data and reply when it has been completed. Sending an email does not immediately delete your account.

3. What will be deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account for Next Brainrot: Evolution.
  • The associated cloud save and game-progress data stored in Cloud Firestore, including linked saved progress and settings.

Deleting an Authentication account alone does not automatically remove Firestore records; the associated cloud-save data must also be removed as part of handling your request.

Deletion is permanent. Once completed, your deleted cloud progress cannot be restored. Signing in again may create a new game account; it will not recover deleted cloud data.

4. Local device data

Local game data is separate from your cloud account. You can remove it using your device's app storage settings (for example, Android Settings → Apps → Next Brainrot: Evolution → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data). Menu names vary by device. If the game offers a local reset option, you can use that as well.

Clearing local data or uninstalling the game does not itself delete your Firebase account or Firestore cloud save. Cloud deletion also does not automatically erase copies already stored on your devices. Clear local game data on each device if you want to remove those copies, and avoid syncing old local progress to a newly created account.

5. Scope and limited retention

This request concerns your account and data for this game. It does not delete your Google account or independently held Google advertising records. Google's handling of its own records is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

We may retain limited correspondence needed to handle and document your request, or information required for a specific legal or security purpose, only for as long as necessary. If an exception affects deletion of your game data, we will explain the relevant scope and reason in our response.
