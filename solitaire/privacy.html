Privacy Policy – Solitaire: All You Need

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how The Little Dynasty Games ("we", "us"), the developer and operator of Solitaire: All You Need (the "game"), handles information. We are responsible for the processing we determine as described here. Our privacy contact is kisskiraly79@gmail.com.

At a glance: Solitaire: All You Need is recommended for players aged 13 and older. You can play offline without creating a game account. Saves and preferences are stored on your device. Optional rewarded test ads can give you one Diamond after a completed view. The game has no real-money purchases. Google advertising/consent services and Unity diagnostics can process technical information when used.

1. The game and this policy

Solitaire: All You Need is a single-player card game being developed for Android and iOS/iPadOS. Its game modes are Classic (Klondike), Spider with one, two or four suits, FreeCell, Pyramid, TriPeaks and Golf. Availability may differ by platform. Playing does not require your name, email address, telephone number, a game account or a social login. The current game has no online multiplayer, chat, public player profile or developer-operated cloud saving.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Game mode and difficulty, shuffled card layouts and deal seeds, card positions, face-up/face-down state, moves, elapsed time and completion state.
  • Move history used by Undo, separate saved games for the supported modes and Spider difficulties, and backup files used to recover a damaged save.
  • Volume levels, animation preferences, table theme and card-back choices.
  • Your Diamond balance and local reward receipts that prevent a completed ad from awarding the same reward twice.

These records allow you to resume games, undo moves and keep your preferences. The game does not upload its save files to a developer-operated server. Your operating system or device backup service may separately copy or restore app data according to your device settings. Local saving does not mean that advertising and consent services, engine diagnostics, support correspondence or this website never involve an internet connection.

3. Unity engine diagnostics

The game is made with Unity. Engine diagnostic data collection is enabled in the current project configuration. Unity may receive technical information about the app, device, software/hardware performance, crashes and errors, including an installation-specific identifier, to help investigate stability and reliability. This is separate from the local card saves described above.

The current game does not enable a separate Unity Analytics service or integrate Firebase Analytics. Google Mobile Ads and UMP are used separately as described below. These choices do not disable Unity engine diagnostics. There is currently no in-game switch for diagnostic collection. Contact us about diagnostic data requests; where appropriate, we will work with Unity to identify the relevant records and handle the request.

Unity describes its role in Diagnostics as a processor acting for the developer, with a default personal-data retention period of 90 days. See Unity's Diagnostics privacy information and the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for the relevant service practices and Unity's own processing.

4. Optional rewarded test ads, privacy choices and purchases

The menu's +1 Diamond button offers an optional rewarded ad through Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. A completed view earns exactly one locally saved Diamond. Closing before the reward is earned, or a loading/presentation failure, does not earn a Diamond. Watching is optional and is not required to play the card games. The current integration uses Google's official test ad units rather than live advertising inventory; Unity Editor uses the SDK's simulated ad.

Google's SDK may handle IP-derived approximate location, device/app identifiers, ad interactions and technical performance or crash information. Processing depends on the platform, SDK configuration and available permissions. Test ads and non-personalized requests do not mean that no data is processed. Read the Android SDK disclosure, iOS SDK disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

This test integration applies Google's most restricted age treatment, a general-audience content filter and a non-personalized-ad request to every player. It does not collect a birth date. On mobile, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether an ad request is permitted before the game initializes the ad SDK and loads an ad. UMP requests use the under-age-of-consent flag for all players in this prototype. If requests are not permitted, ads stay unavailable. These settings are not identity or age verification.

Where Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings → Hirdetési adatvédelem lets you review the available choices. Availability depends on UMP's response. The game also links to this policy from Settings. Device and Google privacy controls may offer additional choices. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it through the applicable controls without affecting earlier lawful processing. See Google's UMP information.

Diamonds currently have no real-money value and cannot be bought, transferred or redeemed for cash. The Shop remains a placeholder; there are no real-money purchases, payment-card collection, real-money betting or cash prizes. Gameplay does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise GPS location. We will review this policy before switching to live ads or adding features that change these practices.

5. Purposes and legal grounds

Local records provide the requested game, preserve progress and preferences, and keep the Diamond balance and rewards consistent. Advertising and consent information supports optional test ads, privacy choices, measurement and fraud prevention. Diagnostic information supports investigating crashes, errors and reliability. If you contact us, we use your correspondence to respond to support questions and privacy requests.

Where European or UK data-protection law applies, relevant grounds can include providing the requested service for necessary game functions, legitimate interests in maintaining reliability and responding to support requests where those interests do not override your rights, consent for advertising-related processing or device access where required, and compliance with legal obligations. Consent must be obtained where applicable law requires it; a legitimate-interest ground does not replace required consent. You may contact us for details about the processing relevant to your request.

6. Providers, sharing and international processing

Google receives information through its advertising and consent services. The game does not send card saves or the Diamond ledger to Google as custom advertising data. Unity can process diagnostic information as described in section 3. Our email provider processes support correspondence. GitHub hosts this policy page. Device backup providers may process app backups if enabled by you. We do not sell local game saves or send them to an advertising provider.

These providers may process information outside your country, including outside the EEA or UK. Their privacy information and applicable data-processing arrangements explain the relevant safeguards. For Unity Diagnostics, see the Unity Data Processing Addendum. Contact us with questions about transfers for which we are responsible.

7. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. Send only what is necessary to explain your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details, identity documents or unnecessary information about a child. Screenshots or diagnostic logs you voluntarily provide may contain additional information; review them before sending.

8. Retention and security

Local saves and preferences remain until app data is removed, subject to backup and restore settings. The game keeps local backup saves as well as the current save. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed for the request and related follow-up, or an applicable legal or security purpose. Diagnostic retention is described in section 3. Google applies its own retention practices to advertising and consent records; see Google's retention information.

Local storage relies on the operating system's app-storage protections. Save-integrity checks help detect corrupted files but are not encryption. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and any backup account.

9. Removing data and making requests

There is currently no in-game delete-all-data button. On Android, use the operating system's app-storage controls to clear the game's storage/data; clearing only the cache is not the same. On iOS/iPadOS, delete the app and its data rather than merely offloading it. Removing app data can permanently remove saved games, preferences, Diamonds and reward receipts. Manage device/cloud backups separately if you do not want an old save restored.

There is no developer game account or cloud-save account to delete. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase a save that exists only on your device. Deleting the app does not automatically erase support correspondence, Google advertising/consent information or diagnostic records already processed. Google provides its own privacy request channels for records it independently controls. For information we control, email the privacy contact below. We may request proportionate details needed to locate the relevant records and, where necessary, work with our service provider.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us about information for which we are responsible. We may need proportionate information to verify a request and will respond within the period required by applicable law. Available rights and lawful exceptions depend on the processing and your jurisdiction.

11. Children and intended audience

The game is intended and recommended for people aged 13 and older (13+) and is not directed at children under 13. This is our intended audience recommendation; it does not replace the age rating shown by an app store or local rules. An age recommendation is not a claim that parental authorization is unnecessary everywhere: some teenagers may need a parent or guardian's involvement under applicable law. The current game does not ask for a date of birth or perform age verification.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal data through the game without authorization required by law, contact us. We will review the situation and take appropriate steps, including working with the relevant provider where necessary. Please do not include unnecessary information about the child.

Planned features are not statements that they already operate. The updated policy will appear here with a new date, with additional notice or consent where required.
